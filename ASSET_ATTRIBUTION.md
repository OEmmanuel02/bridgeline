# Asset attribution

## Fonts

- **DM Sans** — Google Fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Loaded from Google Fonts in `index.html`.
- **Playfair Display** — Google Fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Loaded from Google Fonts in `index.html`.

## Images and illustrations

The Bridgeline wordmark, favicon, and homepage illustrations are original CSS and SVG artwork created for this project. The homepage contains no stock photography, candidate photographs, employer logos, or partner identities.

The CSS artwork does not require separate image licensing. The SVG brand assets are project artwork. Google Fonts are requested from Google's font-hosting service at runtime; the external request and its privacy implications should be included in the site's final vendor/privacy review. No external icon pack is used.
